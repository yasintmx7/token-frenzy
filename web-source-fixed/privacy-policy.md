# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 28, 2026

Welcome to **Token Frenzy**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game on web or mobile devices.

### 1. Information We Collect
We do not require you to create a traditional account (email/password). Instead, we use your public Solana wallet address for identification and access control.

*   **Public Wallet Address:** When you connect your wallet, we receive your public address. We use this to check for **Game Pass NFT** ownership and to identify you on the global leaderboard.
*   **Gameplay Stats & Scores:** We store your high scores, best combos, and tokens sliced in our database (Supabase) to maintain the "Hall of Fame" leaderboard.
*   **On-Chain Data:** We read publicly available data on the Solana blockchain related to your address to verify asset ownership.

### 2. We DO NOT Collect Private Keys
**We never have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or personal passwords.** Your wallet provider (e.g., Phantom, Solflare) handles all signing and security. We will never ask you for your private key.

### 3. Third-Party Services
We use reputable third-party services to power the game:
*   **Supabase:** Stores global leaderboard scores and wallet-linked gameplay stats.
*   **Helius:** Provides the RPC (Remote Procedure Call) infrastructure to communicate with the Solana blockchain.
*   **Pinata / IPFS:** Hosts the game's NFT metadata and media assets.
*   **Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA):** Facilitates secure wallet connections on Android devices.

### 4. How We Use Information
*   To provide gameplay access based on NFT ownership.
*   To display your rank and progress on the "Hall of Fame".
*   To improve game balance and performance.

### 5. Data Security
While we do not store sensitive personal information, we secure the data we do have (scores/wallet addresses) using industry-standard database protocols. Blockchain transactions are immutable and public by design.

### 6. Your Rights
You can choose to stop using the app and disconnect your wallet at any time. Since your scores are linked to your public wallet address, you can request a score removal by contacting us with proof of wallet ownership.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
